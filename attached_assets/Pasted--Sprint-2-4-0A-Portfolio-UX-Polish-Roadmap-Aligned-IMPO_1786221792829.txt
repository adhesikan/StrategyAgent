## CRITICAL ROADMAP RULE

Do not deviate from the established roadmap.

Whenever implementation, UAT, deployment, operations, documentation, monitoring, security, testing, or UX improvements are identified:

• Add them to the most appropriate planned sprint.
• If they are small refinements, place them in an "A" sprint immediately following the related sprint (example: 2.4.0A).
• Never create unrelated roadmap branches.
• Never move future milestones earlier.
• Keep Portfolio Intelligence, AI Analysis, Broker Sync, Rebalancing, Goal Planning, Tax Features, Options Analysis, and Advisor features in their originally planned sprints.
• Update the Roadmap, Operations Manual, Sprint Change Log, and UAT documentation whenever refinements are added.

This rule applies to every future sprint automatically.
