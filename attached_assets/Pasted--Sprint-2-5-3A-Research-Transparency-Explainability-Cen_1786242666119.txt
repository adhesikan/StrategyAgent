4. CANONICAL DEFINITIONS
============================================================

Use clear plain-English definitions.

Do NOT overstate methodology beyond what actually exists in code.

Example:

Research Score

Short:
"A composite score used to rank research candidates."

Full:
"Research Score combines deterministic evidence already produced by the platform, including technical, institutional, fundamental and risk-related inputs. It is used to organize and compare research candidates."

Caution:
"It is not a prediction of future returns, a probability of success, or investment advice."

Technical Score

Short:
"Measures the strength of technical evidence."

Full:
"Measures technical research evidence such as trend quality, price structure, volume behavior, support/resistance and other deterministic technical signals available to the platform."

Institutional Score

Short:
"Measures available institutional evidence."

Full:
"Measures publicly available evidence of institutional participation, ownership trends and related institutional signals. Form 13F information is delayed and does not represent real-time positions."

Fundamental Score

Short:
"Measures available fundamental business evidence."

Full:
"Measures available company fundamentals such as growth, profitability, balance-sheet quality, cash-flow characteristics and related business metrics."

Risk Score

IMPORTANT:
Verify actual score semantics from code before defining this.

If higher score = higher risk:
say exactly that.

If higher score = better risk quality / lower risk:
say exactly that instead.

DO NOT guess.

Evidence Confidence

Short:
"Describes the amount and consistency of supporting research evidence."

Caution:
"It does not represent the probability that a security will rise or fall."
