============================================================
OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Transform the existing admin-only Platform Health page into a true Operations Center.

An operator should be able to answer within seconds:

1. Is the application healthy?
2. Is today's market data current?
3. Has the scanner run?
4. Has ranking completed?
5. Is Opportunity Intelligence current?
6. Are sector/theme intelligence snapshots current?
7. Is Research Monitoring current?
8. Is the Command Center ready?
9. Are Research Reports operational?
10. Are Portfolio History services operational?
11. Are broker integrations healthy?
12. Which subsystem requires attention?
13. What diagnostic or runbook should be used next?

This sprint is read-only operational visibility.

Do not create duplicate business logic.
