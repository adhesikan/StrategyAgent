======================================================================
OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Give users a reliable historical view of:

1. What their portfolio looked like previously.
2. What changed.
3. Which holdings were added, removed, increased, or reduced.
4. How market value and exposure changed.
5. How existing Opportunity Intelligence evidence changed for their holdings.
6. What changed since yesterday / previous snapshot / selected period.

The key user question is:

"What changed in my portfolio?"

NOT:

"What should I buy or sell?"

======================================================================
7. SHARE/QUANTITY CHANGE RULES
======================================================================

Deterministic definitions:

NEW:
current quantity > 0 and previous absent

EXITED:
previous quantity > 0 and current absent

INCREASED:
current quantity > previous quantity

REDUCED:
current quantity < previous quantity and > 0

UNCHANGED:
same quantity

Do not infer trades beyond what the snapshots prove.

Example:

Do not say:
"User bought NVDA."

Say:
"NVDA position increased by 20 shares since the previous snapshot."

This matters for correctness and compliance.
