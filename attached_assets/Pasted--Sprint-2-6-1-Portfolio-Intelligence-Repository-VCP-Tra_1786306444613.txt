======================================================================
PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

VCP Trader AI remains RESEARCH-FIRST.

Portfolio Intelligence is a personalization layer over the existing Research Platform.

Opportunity Intelligence owns:

- researchScore
- technicalScore
- institutionalScore
- fundamentalScore
- risk semantics
- opportunityType
- evidence confidence
- qualification status
- primary evidence
- secondary evidence
- risk factors
- thesis invalidation

Portfolio Intelligence MUST consume those fields.

DO NOT create another scoring universe.

DO NOT independently rank securities.

DO NOT create buy/sell/rebalance recommendations.

======================================================================
OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Help a user understand:

"What does the existing VCP Trader AI research mean for MY portfolio?"

The system should answer:

- Where am I concentrated?
- Which sectors and themes dominate?
- Which holdings have strong research evidence?
- Which holdings have weakened?
- Which holdings overlap with current qualified opportunities?
- Which holdings lack sufficient research coverage?
- Which portfolio exposures have recently changed?
- What deserves further research?

Everything should be descriptive and research-oriented.
