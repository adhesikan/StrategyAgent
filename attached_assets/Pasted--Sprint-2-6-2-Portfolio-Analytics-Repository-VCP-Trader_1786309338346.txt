======================================================================
OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Build a professional Portfolio Analytics experience that helps users visually understand:

1. Portfolio value over time
2. Cost basis vs current market value
3. Gain/loss history where data supports it
4. Position allocation
5. Sector allocation
6. Theme allocation
7. Concentration
8. Exposure changes over time
9. Position history
10. Research coverage trends
11. Opportunity overlap trends
12. Research strengthening / weakening trends

The user should be able to answer:

"How is my portfolio structured?"

"How has it changed?"

"Where is my exposure concentrated?"

"How much of my portfolio is covered by current research?"

"Which research dimensions are improving or weakening?"

NOT:

"What should I buy or sell?"
