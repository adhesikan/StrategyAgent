==============================================================================
PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
==============================================================================

VCP Trader AI is RESEARCH-FIRST.

The Opportunity Workspace should become the single best place in the product to understand one security.

It should answer:

Why is this security currently relevant?

What evidence supports it?

What changed?

Where is evidence strengthening or weakening?

What are the risks?

What would invalidate the thesis?

How does the sector/theme context look?

What institutional evidence exists?

What related research exists?

How fresh is the data?

What should I research next?

It must NOT answer:

"What should I buy?"

"What should I sell?"

"What trade should I place?"

Trade construction belongs to the future Trade Planning phase.
