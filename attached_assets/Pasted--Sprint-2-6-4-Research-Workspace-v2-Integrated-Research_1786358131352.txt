======================================================================
PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

VCP Trader AI is RESEARCH-FIRST.

The Research Workspace should become the cross-platform orchestration layer for:

- Opportunity research
- Company research
- Theme research
- Sector research
- Institutional research
- Market research
- Collection research
- Comparison research
- Portfolio-context research
- Research report follow-up
- Monitoring/change explanation

The AI must work from existing deterministic platform evidence.

It must NOT invent:

- candidates
- scores
- evidence
- qualifications
- institutional activity
- portfolio holdings
- trade structures
- target prices

The AI explains, compares, challenges, summarizes, and organizes existing research.
