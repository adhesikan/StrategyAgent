======================================================================
1. PRODUCT OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Build a reusable Goals & Research Planning layer that allows users to
tell VCP Trader AI what they are trying to research or accomplish.

Examples:

- Long-term capital growth
- Income-oriented research
- Capital preservation
- Long-term compounding
- Dividend-focused research
- Growth-oriented research
- Lower-volatility research
- AI infrastructure research
- Semiconductor research
- Options-income research
- Retirement-oriented research
- Custom research objective

Goals should personalize:

Research Workspace
Opportunity discovery
Collections
Research monitoring
Research reports
Portfolio research
Command Center
Future Trade Planning

Goals must NOT become:

investment recommendations
suitability determinations
fiduciary advice
automated portfolio management
automatic rebalancing
trade execution
trade construction

The goal system describes USER RESEARCH PREFERENCES.

It does not decide what the user should own or trade.
