======================================================================
CORE ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

Research determines WHAT deserves investigation.

Goals personalize WHICH research matters to the user.

Trade Planning determines HOW a qualified research candidate could
potentially be expressed.

Execution happens only after explicit user review and confirmation.

This sprint builds ONLY the bridge between Research and Trade Planning.

It does NOT construct actual trades yet.
