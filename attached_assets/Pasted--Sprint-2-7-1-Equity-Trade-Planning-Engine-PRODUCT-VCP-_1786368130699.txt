======================================================================

Research answers:

WHAT deserves investigation?

WHICH research matters to this user?

HOW does this research relate to existing exposure?

HOW could this qualified thesis potentially be expressed?

======================================================================
OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Build a reusable Equity Trade Planning Engine that converts:

TradePlanningContext
        +
user planning constraints
        +
canonical research evidence
        +
market/reference price context
        ↓
EquityPlanningScenario

The scenario should help a user understand:

- how an equity research thesis could be structured
- what conditions would support entering
- what would invalidate the thesis
- how much capital a hypothetical scenario uses
- how position size changes under different risk assumptions
- what market/research conditions should be monitored
- what happens under several price scenarios

The engine must NOT tell the user:

"You should buy this."

  entryConditionType

======================================================================
7. POSITION SIZING METHODOLOGY
======================================================================

If a validated invalidation price exists:

riskPerShare =
referencePrice - invalidationPrice

hypothetical shares by risk =
maxLossPerPosition / riskPerShare

Also enforce:

capital limit

Never exceed:

capitalAvailable

maxCapitalAtRisk

Use deterministic rounding.

Do not imply the resulting quantity is appropriate or recommended.

Label:

Hypothetical Scenario Size

not:

Recommended Position Size
