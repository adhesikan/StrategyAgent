======================================================================
CORE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT
======================================================================

Research answers:

WHAT deserves investigation?

Goals answer:

WHICH research matters to the user?

Portfolio Intelligence answers:

HOW the research relates to current exposure?

Trade Planning Foundation answers:

HOW the research thesis could potentially be expressed?

Equity Planning answers:

HOW an equity research scenario could be structured.

Options Strategy Matching answers:

WHICH OPTIONS STRATEGY FAMILIES are structurally compatible with the
existing research thesis and user-selected planning constraints.

This sprint MUST NOT select:

- expiration
- strike
- contract
- premium
- spread width
- quantity
- broker order

Those belong to later sprints.

======================================================================
OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Build a reusable deterministic Options Strategy Matching Engine that
takes:

TradePlanningContext
        +
Research Thesis
        +
Research Horizon
        +
Portfolio Context
        +
User Planning Constraints
        +
Existing volatility/event/liquidity context where available
        ↓
OptionsStrategyMatchResult
        ↓
Applicable / Potentially Applicable / Not Applicable strategy families

The engine helps answer:

"Which options research structures are compatible with this thesis?"

NOT:

"Which options trade should I place?"

7. PORTFOLIO REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

Certain strategy families require position context.

Covered Call:

requires existing underlying shares for a true covered structure.

If user does NOT own shares:

status:

NOT_APPLICABLE

Reason:

"No qualifying underlying position is available in the selected portfolio context."

Do not present hypothetical naked calls as covered calls.

Protective Put:

requires owned shares.

Collar:

requires owned shares.

Other strategies may not require current ownership.
