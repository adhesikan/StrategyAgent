CORE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT
======================================================================

Research answers:

WHAT deserves investigation?

Goals answer:

WHICH research matters to this user?

Portfolio Intelligence answers:

HOW does this research relate to current exposure?

Trade Planning Foundation answers:

HOW could this research thesis potentially be expressed?

Options Strategy Matching answers:

WHICH strategy family is structurally compatible?

Options Contract Research answers:

WHICH expirations, strikes, and contracts are worth researching
within the already-selected strategy family?

promote a security into research

choose the strategy family from scratch

rewrite the research thesis

construct a broker order

submit a trade
