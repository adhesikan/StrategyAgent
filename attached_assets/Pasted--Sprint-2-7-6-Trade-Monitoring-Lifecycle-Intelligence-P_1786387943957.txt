CORE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT
======================================================================

Research answers:

WHAT deserves investigation?

Goals answer:

WHICH research matters to the user?

Portfolio Intelligence answers:

HOW the research relates to current exposure?

Trade Planning answers:

HOW the research thesis could potentially be expressed?

Contract Research answers:

WHICH concrete structures are worth researching?

Risk Analysis answers:

WHAT the risk characteristics and scenario behavior are?

Trade Plan Workspace answers:

WHAT research plan the user chose to save?

Trade Monitoring & Lifecycle Intelligence answers:

HAS anything important changed since the plan was saved?

This sprint observes.

It does NOT execute.

It does NOT recommend exits.

It does NOT modify saved trade plans automatically.

======================================================================
OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Build a deterministic lifecycle monitoring layer for saved Trade Plans.

It should answer:

- Has the underlying research thesis changed?
- Has qualification changed?
- Has technical evidence changed?
- Have fundamentals changed?
- Has institutional evidence changed?
- Has sector/theme leadership changed?
- Has market regime changed?
- Has a saved thesis invalidation condition been observed?
- Has options-specific structure risk changed?
- Has event exposure changed?
- Has liquidity/quote quality changed?
- Has data gone stale?
- Does this plan require research review?

The system may say:

"Research Review Required"

It must NOT say:

"Exit the trade"

======================================================================
4. STATE RULES
======================================================================

CURRENT

No material research or structure change.

CHANGED

Research/evidence changed but no review threshold crossed.

REQUIRES_REVIEW

Material change detected based on existing change rules.

THESIS_INVALIDATED

One or more canonical saved invalidation conditions have been observed.

DATA_STALE

Critical required data is too stale to safely evaluate current state.

ARCHIVED

Plan inactive.

UNKNOWN

Insufficient data.

Do not create new arbitrary thresholds if Change Intelligence already owns them.
