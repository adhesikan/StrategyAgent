PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

This sprint is a RELEASE CERTIFICATION sprint.

Do NOT use it to add major product features.

Validate the complete VCP Trader AI platform as one integrated system before
Phase 2.8 introduces broker-assisted execution.

The platform must demonstrate that the following complete flow works:

Discover
→ Research
→ Explain
→ Personalize
→ Portfolio Context
→ Trade Planning
→ Equity / Options Structure Research
→ Contract Research
→ Risk Analysis
→ Save Trade Plan
→ Lifecycle Monitoring

This sprint answers:

"Is VCP Trader AI Research + Trade Intelligence & Planning v1.0 ready to
operate reliably in production?"
