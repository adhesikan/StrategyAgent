======================================================================
PRIMARY PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

The user should decide HOW they want to explore or express a qualified
research opportunity.

VCP Trader AI should NOT make the top-level instrument/strategy decision
for the user.

Preferred hierarchy:

Qualified Research Candidate
        ↓
USER chooses broad expression category
        ↓
VCP evaluates compatible structures INSIDE that category
        ↓
USER chooses specific structure
        ↓
Contract Research where applicable
        ↓
Risk Analysis
        ↓
Trade Plan
        ↓
Execution Preflight
        ↓
Order Preparation
        ↓
Order Preview
        ↓
Future Explicit Confirmation
        ↓
Future Broker Submission

This sprint implements the USER-DIRECTED EXPRESSION SELECTION layer.
