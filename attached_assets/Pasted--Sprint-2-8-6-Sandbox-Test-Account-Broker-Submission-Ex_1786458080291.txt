submission of EXACTLY ONE immutable confirmed snapshot.

CRITICAL SAFETY RULE
======================================================================

If the broker response is ambiguous and VCP Trader AI cannot determine
whether the order was accepted:

DO NOT RETRY AUTOMATICALLY.

Enter:

SUBMISSION_UNKNOWN

Then reconcile against the broker using known request/client/order
identifiers before deciding whether any new submission is permitted.

This invariant is mandatory.
