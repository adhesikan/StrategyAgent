Homepage Conversion Update — Outcome-First Messaging for Active Traders

CONTEXT

VCP Trader AI is already built and deployed on Replit/Railway.

The current homepage has:

- strong dark-theme design
- polished cards and layouts
- hero section
- stock and options sections
- AI scenario grades
- risk controls
- InstaTrade™
- pricing
- FAQ
- responsive React components

The visual design is already good.

This task is NOT a redesign.

The goal is to improve visitor-to-trial conversion by changing the messaging hierarchy from:

feature-first

to:

outcome-first

The homepage should appeal to active traders who currently look for:

- stock signals
- options ideas
- swing setups
- income trades
- watchlist opportunities
- market alerts
- newsletter trade ideas
- scanner results

The product must still be presented as a self-directed decision-support platform, not as personalized investment advice.

Do not commit, push, or deploy automatically.

==================================================
1. PRIMARY GOAL
==================================================

A new visitor should understand within 10 seconds that VCP Trader AI helps them:

- discover stock and options opportunities
- find growth and income ideas
- understand why a setup qualifies
- review risk before acting
- prepare an order through a connected broker

Do not lead with the word “research.”

Research may remain in legal, disclosure, saved-record, and technical contexts, but it should not be the primary marketing message.

Prefer:

- opportunities
- trade ideas
- setups
- candidates
- market intelligence
- opportunity discovery
- trade review
- watchlist intelligence

6. ADD “HOW IT FINDS OPPORTUNITIES”
==================================================

After the opportunity preview, add or reorganize a section:

Headline:

How VCP Trader AI Finds Opportunities

Use concise cards or a simple workflow:

1. Scan

Monitors technical setups, watchlists, market conditions, and options structures.

2. Qualify

Applies deterministic rules to separate qualified, developing, rejected, and unavailable setups.

3. Rank

Surfaces higher-quality candidates using strategy strength, freshness, market context, risk, and confluence.

4. Explain

Summarizes why a setup qualifies or does not qualify in plain English.

5. Review Risk

Shows invalidation, reward/risk, capital requirements, liquidity, and portfolio impact.

6. Prepare

Lets the user review a broker-ready order through InstaTrade™ with explicit confirmation.

Keep this section concise.
