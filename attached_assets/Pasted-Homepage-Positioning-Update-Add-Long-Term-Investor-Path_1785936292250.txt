Homepage Positioning Update — Add Long-Term Investor Path to the Existing Outcome-First Build

CONTEXT

The VCP Trader AI homepage has already been updated from feature-first messaging to outcome-first messaging for active stock and options traders.

The current build emphasizes:

- daily opportunities
- growth ideas
- income ideas
- trade setups
- opportunity grades
- market context
- risk review
- InstaTrade™
- self-directed decision making

The visual design is already strong and should remain unchanged.

GOAL

Expand the existing homepage positioning so VCP Trader AI clearly serves both:

1. active traders seeking stock and options setups
2. long-term investors seeking growth opportunities, thesis monitoring, disciplined entry conditions, and portfolio intelligence

Do not turn the homepage into a generic investing site.

Do not weaken the appeal to active traders.

Instead, organize the product around four user goals:

- Grow
- Generate Income
- Trade
- Understand Markets

This remains an AI-powered opportunity discovery and decision-support platform for self-directed users.

Do not commit, push, or deploy automatically.

9. UPDATE “HOW VCP TRADER AI FINDS OPPORTUNITIES”
==================================================

Keep the existing opportunity workflow but broaden it slightly.

Use:

1. Discover

Scans stocks, options, themes, sectors, and market conditions.

2. Evaluate

Applies deterministic technical, market, event, and portfolio criteria.

3. Qualify

Separates qualified, developing, rejected, and unavailable opportunities.

4. Explain

Shows the evidence, risks, limitations, and what conditions matter.

5. Monitor

Tracks saved ideas, watchlists, thesis changes, earnings, and market conditions.

6. Prepare

Helps users review a stock or options order through a connected broker with explicit confirmation.

Do not mention internal MCP architecture.
