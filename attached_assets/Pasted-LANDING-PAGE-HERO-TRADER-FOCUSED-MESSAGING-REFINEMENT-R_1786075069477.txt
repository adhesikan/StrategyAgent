LANDING PAGE HERO — TRADER-FOCUSED MESSAGING REFINEMENT

Repository:
VCP Trader AI / StrategyAgent

OBJECTIVE

The revised landing page currently leads with the word “Research”:

“Research, Plan, and Verify Stock & Options Opportunities”

This is accurate, but it may feel too academic and passive for active traders who are
primarily looking for qualified opportunities, actionable setups, trade structures,
risk levels, and broker verification.

Update the public landing-page messaging so it feels more opportunity-driven and
trader-oriented while preserving compliance.

This is a focused marketing-copy and presentation refinement only.

Do not modify:

- product logic
- scanner
- Opportunity Engine
- AI Trading Workspace
- broker integrations
- Live Contract Resolver
- InstaTrade™ functionality
- pricing
- authentication
- database
- APIs
- feature flags

====================================================================
1. HERO HEADLINE
====================================================================

Replace the current hero headline:

“Research, Plan, and Verify Stock & Options Opportunities”

with:

“Find, Evaluate, and Plan Stock & Options Opportunities”

Keep “Stock & Options Opportunities” visually emphasized using the existing blue
treatment.

Alternative permitted only if it fits the layout better:

“Turn Market Opportunities Into Structured Trade Plans”

Use the first option by default.

- AI Trade Recommendations
- AI Stock Picks
- Winning Opportunities
- High-Probability Trades
- Predictive Trading Signals

3. HERO SUPPORTING COPY
====================================================================

Use:

“Discover qualified setups, understand the evidence and risks, compare stock and
options structures, verify live contracts through supported brokerages, and prepare

Discover
→ Understand
→ Compare
→ Verify
→ Review

Do not describe VCP Trader AI as providing personalized recommendations.

9. COMPLIANCE
====================================================================

The stronger opportunity language must remain clearly non-advisory.

Retain contextual wording such as:

- qualified by deterministic rules
- illustrative structure

- opportunities are suitable for the user
- qualification predicts returns
- a setup is actionable without verification
- AI determines what the user should buy or sell
