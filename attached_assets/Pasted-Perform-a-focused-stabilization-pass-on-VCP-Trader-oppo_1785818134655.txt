CURRENT ISSUES

Observed examples:

- "Find three bullish trades" returns three scanner setups, but all are marked NO_TRADE.
- The headline still says "Three bullish trade ideas identified."
- Recommendation requests sometimes show Rejected: 1 even though many setups or strategies were evaluated.
- Market-wide requests do not clearly distinguish:
  - scanner setup
  - watch candidate
  - qualified trade candidate
  - rejected candidate
  - unavailable data

GOAL

Make all counts, labels, headlines, and cards semantically truthful.

==================================================
1. DEFINE RESULT TYPES
==================================================

Use explicit categories:

ACTIONABLE_TRADE
WATCH
SCANNER_SETUP
REJECTED
UNAVAILABLE

A scanner setup is not automatically a trade idea.

A setup may become:

- ACTIONABLE_TRADE after candidate and risk qualification
- WATCH when developing or missing confirmation
- REJECTED when candidate qualification fails
- UNAVAILABLE when qualification cannot run

==================================================
2. FIX HEADLINES
==================================================

Examples:

Three actionable:
"Three bullish trade candidates identified."

Mixed:
"Three bullish setups reviewed; one currently qualifies as a trade."

All watch:
"Three bullish setups are worth monitoring, but none are actionable yet."

All rejected:
"Three bullish setups were found, but none currently qualify as trades."

No results:
"No qualifying bullish setups currently meet the criteria."

Never call NO_TRADE cards "trade ideas."

==================================================
3. FIX COUNTS
==================================================

Expose truthful counts:

{
  setupsReviewed,
  actionable,
  watch,
  rejected,
  unavailable
}

For market-wide recommendation searches, counts must describe candidate qualification results.

For symbol analysis, counts may describe strategies evaluated.

Do not mix strategy counts and opportunity counts.

==================================================
4. CARD LABELS
==================================================

Use:

TRADE
WATCH
SETUP
NO TRADE
UNAVAILABLE

Do not use NO_TRADE as a secondary badge while the page headline calls the same item a trade idea.

==================================================
5. NARRATIVE
==================================================

The AI narrative must use the same result categories and counts.

Examples:

"Reviewed 3 scanner setups. None passed candidate qualification."

"Reviewed 5 setups. One qualified, two are watch candidates, and two were rejected."
