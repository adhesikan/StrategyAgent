3. DISTINGUISH TRIGGER SEMANTICS
==================================================

Determine whether each strategy uses:

A. explicit price trigger

C. event trigger

- Volume Surge may use a volume/price confirmation event
- VWAP Reclaim may use a reclaim level

Do not force all strategies into a single price-trigger model if their semantics differ.
