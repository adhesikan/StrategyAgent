4. HEADLINE SEMANTICS
==================================================

Do not claim every candidate failed the risk limit unless the backend explicitly confirms that risk was the terminal rejection reason.

Use deterministic headline precedence:

A. Qualified candidates exist:
"<N> candidates qualify under the $500 risk constraint."

B. No qualified, but candidates reached qualification and were rejected primarily for risk:
"No candidate met the $500 maximum-risk limit."

C. Candidates were mostly excluded before qualification or unavailable:
"No candidate currently qualifies for the $500 risk request."

D. All candidates unavailable:
"Candidates could not be qualified because required market data was unavailable."

E. No opportunities:
"No opportunities were available for evaluation."

Derive this only from backend counts and reason codes.

Do not infer that an excluded or unavailable candidate failed the risk test.
