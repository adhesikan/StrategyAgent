SPRINT 2.0.1 — Dashboard UX Polish & Opportunity Command Center

IMPORTANT

This sprint is UI/UX only.

DO NOT modify:

- Opportunity Engine
- MCP
- Scanner algorithms
- MarketHistoryService
- PostgreSQL schema
- Broker integrations
- Opportunity Lifecycle logic
- APIs
- Persistence
- Ranking
- Confidence calculations

Assume Sprint 2.0 backend is production-ready.

The objective is to transform the dashboard into an institutional-quality trading command center.

======================================================
PRIMARY GOAL
======================================================

A trader should understand the market in less than 10 seconds.

The dashboard should answer:

1. What is today's market condition?
2. What opportunities deserve immediate attention?
3. What changed since yesterday?
4. What should I watch next?
5. What requires action?

Prioritize information hierarchy over adding more information.
