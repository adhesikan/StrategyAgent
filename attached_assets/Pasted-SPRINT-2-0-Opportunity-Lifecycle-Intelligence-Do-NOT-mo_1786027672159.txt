SPRINT 2.0 – Opportunity Lifecycle Intelligence

Do NOT modify any broker integrations, MCP server APIs, database-first market history architecture, Opportunity Engine scheduling, or existing scanner algorithms.

Everything built in Sprint 1 is now considered production architecture.

The objective of this sprint is NOT to find more opportunities.

The objective is to make every opportunity far more valuable by tracking how it evolves over time.

==================================================================
GOAL
==================================================================

Instead of only displaying today's ranked opportunities, build a complete lifecycle tracking system.

A trader should immediately understand:

• What is NEW today
• What became stronger
• What weakened
• What triggered
• What disappeared
• What should remain on the watchlist
• How today's scan compares to yesterday

The Opportunity Engine becomes a continuously evolving research engine rather than a one-time scanner.
