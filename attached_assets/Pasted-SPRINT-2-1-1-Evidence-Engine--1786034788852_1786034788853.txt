SPRINT 2.1.1 – Evidence Engine

====================================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================================

Build a unified Evidence Engine that aggregates every research source
supporting an Opportunity Engine candidate.

This is NOT another scanner.

This is NOT another dashboard.

This is a structured evidence system.

Every qualified research candidate should become an institutional-quality
research dossier.

The Evidence Engine must support multiple evidence providers while keeping
one consistent user experience.

Everything presented must be factual, deterministic or clearly attributed.

Never fabricate information.

Never generate unsupported claims.

====================================================================
DO NOT MODIFY
====================================================================

Do NOT modify

• Opportunity Engine
• MCP ranking
• Lifecycle engine
• Opportunity scoring
• Broker integrations
• MarketHistoryService
• Database-first architecture
• Scanner algorithms
• Portfolio engine
• Existing APIs

This sprint extends the research experience only.

====================================================================
ARCHITECTURE
====================================================================

Create

EvidenceEngine
