SPRINT 2.1 – Research Package Experience

====================================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================================

Transform every Opportunity Engine candidate into a complete Research Package.

The goal is NOT to generate more opportunities.

The goal is to help a self-directed trader understand WHY the opportunity qualified, WHAT evidence supports it, HOW they might research a stock or options trade, and WHEN they may decide to execute using InstaTrade™ if connected to a brokerage.

This sprint is about research, education, planning and transparency.

Never present investment recommendations.

Never present personalized investment advice.

Never fabricate data.

====================================================================
DO NOT CHANGE
====================================================================

Do NOT modify:

• Opportunity Engine
• MCP
• Scanner algorithms
• MarketHistoryService
• Broker integrations
• Portfolio engine
• Opportunity Lifecycle
• Opportunity scoring
• PostgreSQL schema (unless absolutely required)
• Existing API contracts

Only extend the research experience.

====================================================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
====================================================================

When a trader opens a Research Package they should understand

1. Why this opportunity exists.

2. Why today.

3. What evidence supports it.

4. What risks exist.

5. How they could research a stock position.

6. How they could research an options position.

7. When InstaTrade™ becomes available.

The page should feel like a professional investment research terminal rather than a stock scanner.
