SPRINT 2.2.4 — AI Trading Workspace UX Simplification

Repository:
VCP Trader AI

OBJECTIVE

The current AI Trading Workspace contains excellent information but presents too much simultaneously.

The goal of this sprint is NOT to remove functionality.

The goal is to redesign the information hierarchy so traders naturally move through:

Understand
↓

Plan

↓

Verify

↓

Execute (InstaTrade™)

The experience should feel like an institutional trading workstation with AI acting as the analyst—not a long research report.

NO server changes.

NO database changes.

NO API changes.

NO MCP changes.

NO scanner changes.

NO business logic changes.

NO deterministic scoring changes.

NO option calculations changed.

UI / UX ONLY.

--------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY DESIGN PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------

Reduce cognitive load.

Show only what matters first.

Everything else should progressively disclose.

One primary CTA per screen.

Professional institutional look.

Fast scanning.

Less scrolling.

Cleaner typography.

Better whitespace.
