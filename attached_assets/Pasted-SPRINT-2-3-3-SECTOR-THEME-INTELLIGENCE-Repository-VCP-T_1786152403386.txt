CURRENT STATE

Completed:

- Opportunity Research Workspace

- Institutional Fund Explorer

OBJECTIVE

Build a deterministic Sector & Theme Intelligence layer that aggregates existing stock-level
evidence into sector- and theme-level research intelligence.

The user should be able to answer:

- Which sectors are strengthening?
- Which themes have the highest-quality opportunities?
- Which themes are seeing broader institutional accumulation?
- Which themes are weakening?
- Which individual stocks are driving a theme?
- What changed since the previous scan?

This is a research feature.

Do NOT provide investment recommendations.
Do NOT modify scanner logic.
Do NOT modify Opportunity Ranking weights.
Do NOT introduce LLM-generated scores.

============================================================
6. BREADTH
============================================================

Create deterministic breadth measures.

Examples:

technicalBreadth =
percentage of ranked theme members with technicalScore >= threshold

institutionalBreadth =
percentage of mapped theme members with institutional accumulation evidence

opportunityBreadth =
percentage of members currently ranked Top Growth / Income / Watch as appropriate

Expose numerator and denominator.

Never hide missing-data denominator effects.

Return:

"AI Infrastructure strengthened as 5 members improved and 2 entered the ranked opportunity set."

No LLM.
