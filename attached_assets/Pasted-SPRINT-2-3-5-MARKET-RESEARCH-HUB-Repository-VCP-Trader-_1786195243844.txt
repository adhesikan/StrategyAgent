OBJECTIVE

Create a unified Market Research Hub where traders can discover and navigate all
major research surfaces from one place.

This is NOT another scoring engine.

Do NOT duplicate calculations.

Do NOT redesign the main dashboard.

Do NOT introduce personalized recommendations.

The Hub should organize existing intelligence into a clean research workflow.

============================================================
1. NEW PAGE
============================================================

Create:

/research

Title:

Market Research

Positioning:

"Explore opportunities, market leadership, institutional activity, and important
changes across the market."

Keep the page compact and trader-oriented.

============================================================
2. PRIMARY RESEARCH MODULES
============================================================

Create six primary modules:

A. Opportunities
B. Market Intelligence
C. Institutional Activity
D. Funds
E. Changes
F. Events

Each module should reuse existing APIs and engines.

No duplicated computation.
