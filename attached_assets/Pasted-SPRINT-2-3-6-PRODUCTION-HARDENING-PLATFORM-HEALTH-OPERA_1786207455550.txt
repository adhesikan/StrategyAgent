4. SECTOR FALLBACK POLICY
======================================================================

If a ranked symbol lacks sector metadata:

do not silently discard it.

Classify as:

Unclassified

for diagnostics/coverage purposes.

BUT:

Unclassified must NOT become a normal leading sector.

Expose classification coverage.

Example:

Technology
score 74
coverage high

Unclassified
3 symbols
not scored

Do not produce false sector intelligence from unknown classifications.
