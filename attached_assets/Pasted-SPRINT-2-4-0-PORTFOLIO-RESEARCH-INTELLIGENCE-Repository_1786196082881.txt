OBJECTIVE

Build a compliance-conscious Portfolio Research Intelligence layer.

The system should help a self-directed investor understand:

- What do I own?
- Where is my portfolio concentrated?
- Which holdings currently have strong or weak research evidence?
- What changed in my holdings?
- What sectors and themes dominate my portfolio?
- Which events require research attention?

It must NOT tell the user:

- what to buy
- what to sell
- how much to buy/sell
- what position to replace
- what allocation they "should" have
- what is suitable for their age/income/risk tolerance

This is research and analytics, not personalized investment advice.
