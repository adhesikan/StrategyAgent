4. HOW IT WORKS
====================================================================

Create one clean six-step workflow.

Section headline:

How VCP Trader AI Turns Research Into a Structured Trade Plan

Supporting copy:

A transparent workflow for different goals, strategies, and time horizons.

Step 1 — Discover

VCP Trader AI scans supported market data and surfaces qualified growth, income,
trading, watchlist, and approaching-qualification research candidates.

Step 2 — Understand

Open the AI Trading Workspace to see why a candidate qualified, its lifecycle state,
market regime, rank, evidence, and active risks.

Step 3 — Evaluate

Review technical context, news, catalysts, congressional disclosures, available
institutional context, and thesis invalidation conditions.

Step 4 — Plan

Compare possible stock and illustrative options structures, including holding period,
DTE framework, strike-selection framework, advantages, and trade-offs.

Step 5 — Verify

With a supported brokerage connection, resolve actual listed expirations and strikes,
inspect available quotes, liquidity, and Greeks, and compare verified contract
candidates.

Step 6 — Review

Prepare the selected stock or options structure for review through InstaTrade™.
Nothing is submitted without explicit user confirmation.

Do not say:

Reviewed order

if the full order-review workflow is not yet production complete.

Preferred wording:

Prepared for review

11. FAQ
====================================================================

Retain the FAQ but update answers for current product reality.

Required questions:

1. Do I need a brokerage account to start?

Answer:

No. You can use Opportunity Research, the AI Trading Workspace, stock and illustrative
options planning, delayed or daily-close market context, and supported research tools
without connecting a broker. A broker is needed for live contract verification,
account context, and broker-connected review capabilities.

2. Does VCP Trader AI include live market data?

Answer:

The platform includes daily-close or stored market data for research. Current quotes,
live options chains, and broker-specific market data require a supported connected
brokerage and appropriate account entitlements.

3. What can I do without connecting a broker?

Answer:

Discover research candidates, review evidence, compare stock and illustrative options
structures, explore DTE and strike frameworks, save research, use supported Congress,
news, catalyst, and market-context tools, and ask contextual questions with VCP AI.

4. Does the trial include paper trading?

Answer based only on actual product capability.

Do not claim paper trading if it is not implemented.

5. Does VCP Trader AI trade automatically?

Answer:

No. VCP Trader AI supports research, planning, verification, and user-directed order
review. It does not autonomously place trades or manage a user’s assets.

6. Is this investment advice?

Answer:

No. VCP Trader AI provides educational research, analysis, and user-directed planning
tools. Users remain responsible for their own decisions and should independently
verify all data.

7. Can I use it for long-term investing?

Answer:

Yes. The platform supports long-term thesis research, growth and earnings context,
valuation context, technical conditions, portfolio concentration, and thesis
monitoring, in addition to active trading workflows.

8. Which brokers are supported?

Use capability-qualified current production wording.

Do not list future integrations as available.

9. Can I cancel anytime?

Ensure answer matches actual billing policy.

10. What is Institutional Intelligence?

Answer:

Institutional Intelligence is a rolling-out research capability based on delayed SEC
Form 13F filings. Coverage may be limited while data, amendments, and security mappings
are validated.
