Sprint 2.5.0 — Opportunity Intelligence Engine

IMPORTANT

Follow the existing roadmap.

This sprint comes immediately after Sprint 2.4.2.

Do NOT implement future Portfolio Intelligence, Portfolio Scoring, Rebalancing, Tax Planning, Goal Planning or AI Portfolio Conversations.

Those remain in their planned future roadmap sprints.

=========================================
OBJECTIVE
=========================================

VCP Trader AI is a Research Intelligence platform.

Its primary purpose is helping users discover, qualify, understand and monitor investment and trading opportunities.

The Opportunity Intelligence Engine becomes the central research engine consumed by every major feature.

Portfolio is NOT the center.

Portfolio consumes this engine later.

=========================================
DESIGN GOALS
=========================================

Every user receives value immediately.

No broker required.

No portfolio required.

No uploaded files required.

Research-first.

Evidence-first.

Compliance-first.

=========================================
CREATE
=========================================

Build a reusable Opportunity Intelligence Engine.

The engine should expose a normalized opportunity model consumed by:

Dashboard

Research

Ask AI

Intelligence

Future Portfolio Intelligence

Future Watchlists

Future Alerts

Future AI Agents

Do NOT duplicate logic across pages.
