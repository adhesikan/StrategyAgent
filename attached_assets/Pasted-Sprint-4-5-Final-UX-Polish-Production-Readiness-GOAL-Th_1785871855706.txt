====================================================
1. REMOVE EMPTY-STATE CARD
====================================================

The current page renders an EmptyState-style card even though the deterministic engine successfully evaluated opportunities.

Replace it with a Deterministic Engine Summary card.

Example:

----------------------------------------------------

Deterministic Engine Result

50 stored opportunities reviewed

46 post-confluence candidates evaluated

0 candidates satisfied every required qualification gate

43 excluded before qualification

3 unavailable due to missing market data

The engine intentionally returned no trade rather than lowering its standards.

----------------------------------------------------

Do not use generic "No setups found" wording.
