Sprint 5.4E — Final Analysis UI Polish

Goal

Finalize the analysis experience for production.

This sprint is presentation-only.

Do NOT change:

• scanner logic
• qualification rules
• confidence logic
• MCP
• trade ranking
• research evidence
• persistence
• GPT routing
• broker integration

Only improve wording, layout, terminology, consistency and readability.

8. IMPROVE WHY IT IS NOT ACTIONABLE
--------------------------------------------------

Convert technical rejection text into plain English.

Current:

no actionable pivot.

Instead:

Current:

Historical high is not an entry pivot.

Instead:

The previous high is historical reference only and does not represent a current breakout opportunity.

Maintain deterministic accuracy.

--------------------------------------------------
9. CLEAN GPT OUTPUT
--------------------------------------------------

Require GPT to:

Use complete sentences.

Avoid repeating identical facts.

Avoid repeating scores.

Avoid repeating price levels already displayed above.

Never restate every strategy.

Instead summarize.

Example:

Three strategies confirmed bullish conditions while four others remain under development.

Never fabricate missing information.

--------------------------------------------------

It is intended to support your own investment research and is not personalized investment advice.

Do not increase legal wording.

Improve readability only.
