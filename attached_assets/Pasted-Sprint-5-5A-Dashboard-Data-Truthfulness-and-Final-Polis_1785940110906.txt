==================================================
3. MARKET SNAPSHOT LABEL
==================================================

The current dashboard displays:

- a global banner saying data is daily data/latest close
- a Market Snapshot badge saying “Live”

This is contradictory unless the Market Snapshot endpoint is genuinely real-time.

If the source is daily-close or delayed:

replace “Live” with:

Latest daily close

or:

Delayed market snapshot

Include the data timestamp.

If it is genuinely current live data from a supported source, preserve “Live.”

Do not infer live status merely because the market is open.

Market status and data freshness are separate concepts.
