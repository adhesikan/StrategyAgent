============================================================
OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Formalize the ability to calculate theoretical call and put values using
broker-independent underlying stock data even when NO actual options-market
quote/chain is available.

This mode must remain clearly separate from observed options-market data.

============================================================
1. INPUTS
============================================================

Design the theoretical pricing engine around:

S = current underlying stock price
K = hypothetical/selected strike
T = time to expiration
r = risk-free rate
q = dividend yield
sigma = volatility assumption
optionType = CALL | PUT

Primary underlying data source:

Twelve Data or canonical broker-independent MarketDataService.

Volatility may be calculated internally from historical underlying prices:

HV10
HV20
HV30
HV60
HV90

Define a canonical default volatility lookback for model calculations and
allow the calculation metadata to identify the lookback used.

Do not assume implied volatility when no actual option-market price exists.

============================================================
2. THEORETICAL PRICING
============================================================

Use the pricing architecture already approved in Audit C:

dividend-adjusted Black-Scholes as Phase 1.

Outputs:

MODEL_CALL_VALUE
MODEL_PUT_VALUE

or equivalent canonical types.

These are theoretical research values.

They MUST NEVER be represented as:

market price
option quote
bid
