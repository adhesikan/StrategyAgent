============================================================
OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Design the maximum broker-independent options research capability for VCP Trader AI.

The product goal is:

Users should be able to perform as much options research, strategy analysis,
contract research, scenario analysis, and Trade Plan creation as technically
possible WITHOUT connecting a brokerage account.

Brokerage should primarily add:

- account-specific permissions
- buying power
- actual positions
- execution validation
- order submission
- order status/fills

Options research itself should not be broker-gated wherever an independent
market-data source or deterministic calculation can provide the required inputs.

15. COVERED CALL / PROTECTIVE PUT OWNERSHIP
============================================================

Important distinction:

Researching a covered-call structure does not necessarily require broker positions.

Broker-independent mode could say:

"Requires 100 shares per contract for execution."

If user manually declares holdings/portfolio data, ownership can be evaluated
against that stored portfolio context.

Broker sync can confirm actual ownership.

Define states:

OWNERSHIP_CONFIRMED_BROKER
OWNERSHIP_CONFIRMED_PORTFOLIO
OWNERSHIP_NOT_CONFIRMED

Do not permit brokerless research to silently become naked-call execution.
