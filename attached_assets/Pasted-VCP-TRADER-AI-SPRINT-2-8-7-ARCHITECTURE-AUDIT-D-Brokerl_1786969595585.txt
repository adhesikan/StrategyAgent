============================================================
OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Redesign the VCP Trader AI user experience around this product principle:

A brokerage connection is OPTIONAL for research and planning.

Users should be able to:

Find opportunities
→ Research
→ Build a Trade Plan
→ Analyze risk/scenarios
→ Monitor the plan

without connecting a broker.

Broker connection should become relevant primarily when the user wants:

account-aware information
or
direct execution.

The application must not feel incomplete, broken, blocked, or degraded merely
because the user has not connected a broker.

============================================================
19. ERROR VS LIMITATION
============================================================

Create UX taxonomy:

ERROR
Something failed unexpectedly.

BLOCKER
User cannot complete the intended action.

LIMITATION
Feature is available with reduced data/capability.

ENHANCEMENT_AVAILABLE
Optional connection/data can enrich the result.

INFORMATION
Neutral context.

Broker absence during research = ENHANCEMENT_AVAILABLE.

Broker absence during direct execution = BLOCKER.
