============================================================
3. BLACK-SCHOLES-MERTON PRICING ENGINE
============================================================

Implement dividend-adjusted Black-Scholes-Merton for theoretical research.

Inputs:

S = underlying price
K = strike
T = time to expiration in calendar-year fraction
r = risk-free rate
q = continuous dividend yield
sigma = volatility input
optionType = CALL | PUT

IMPORTANT TIME CONVENTION

Do NOT use:

T = DTE / 252

for Black-Scholes time to expiry.

Use calendar-time fraction, e.g.:

T = DTE / 365

or a more precise actual-calendar-time fraction consistent with the project's
date utilities.

252 is used for annualizing trading-day realized volatility, not for contract
time-to-expiration.

Document this explicitly.
