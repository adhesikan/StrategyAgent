============================================================
SCOPE DECISION
============================================================

Independent observed-options providers such as:

- MarketData.app
- Polygon/Massive
- other OPRA providers

are DEFERRED beyond V1.

Do not delete the Audit C provider-neutral architecture.

Do not delete IndependentOptionsProvider design work.

Preserve it as future architecture.

Reason for deferral:

- V1 already has meaningful brokerless options research
- actual contract data becomes valuable primarily closer to execution
- broker connections already provide that data
- avoid OPRA redistribution complexity
- avoid additional commercial licensing
- avoid additional provider cost
- avoid unnecessary API/cache/provider complexity
- prioritize higher-value broker-independence work
