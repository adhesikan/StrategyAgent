VCP Trader AI — Trade Plan Lifecycle Bug Fix

We have discovered an important lifecycle defect while testing the existing
NVDA Trade Plan.

DO NOT start a new sprint or add unrelated features.
This is a blocker/bug fix in the current Trade Planning / Execution Preflight
workflow.

CURRENT OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

A saved NVDA Trade Plan was successfully created earlier.

NVDA has subsequently dropped out of the latest Opportunity Intelligence
qualified-candidate snapshot.

Opportunity Intelligence now correctly shows:

NVDA
REMOVED
"NVDA dropped out of the ranking — it no longer meets qualification criteria."

However, this causes two incorrect downstream behaviors:

1. Clicking "Open Research Workspace" from the saved NVDA Trade Plan shows:

   "NVDA — Research Not Available
    This symbol is not present in the latest Opportunity Intelligence snapshot."

2. In another Trade Plan navigation path the application shows:

   "Trade plan not found."

This is incorrect.

CORE PRODUCT RULE

A saved Trade Plan is a persistent historical research record.

It MUST NOT disappear, become inaccessible, or be treated as nonexistent
because the symbol later drops out of the current Opportunity Intelligence
qualified-candidate list.

Current qualification and historical persistence are separate concepts.

EXPECTED DOMAIN MODEL

There are three distinct objects/states:

1. Saved Trade Plan
   Persistent user-owned record.
   Must remain accessible until explicitly archived/deleted by the user.

2. Research Snapshot at Plan Creation
   Immutable historical evidence used when the plan was created.
   Must remain viewable even if current research disappears.

3. Current Opportunity Intelligence State
   Dynamic current-state information.
   The symbol may currently be qualified, changed, removed, or unavailable.

Do not conflate these.

REQUIRED FIX

1. TRADE PLAN PERSISTENCE

Inspect all Trade Plan fetch/navigation logic.

A Trade Plan lookup must be based on the persistent Trade Plan ID and
authenticated user ownership.

It must NOT depend on the symbol currently existing in the Opportunity
Intelligence candidate list.

If the Trade Plan exists in the database, the Trade Plan page must render.

"Trade plan not found" should only appear when the persistent plan genuinely
does not exist or is inaccessible to the authenticated user.

2. LIFECYCLE EVALUATION

When the saved Trade Plan symbol is no longer present in the latest qualified
candidate snapshot, lifecycle evaluation should return a deterministic state
such as:

REQUIRES_REVIEW

with a reason such as:

SYMBOL_NO_LONGER_QUALIFIED

Display:

Research Review Required

NVDA no longer qualifies in the latest Opportunity Intelligence snapshot.
Review the original research thesis against current conditions before
continuing.

This should be a material research change.

Do NOT silently invalidate or delete the Trade Plan.

3. HISTORICAL RESEARCH WORKSPACE

"Open Research Workspace" from a saved Trade Plan must still provide useful
research context.

Preferred behavior:

If current qualified research exists:
    open current research and allow comparison with saved research.

If current qualified research does NOT exist:
    open a historical/saved research view sourced from the immutable research
    snapshot stored with the Trade Plan.

The page should clearly distinguish:

    Research at Plan Creation

from:

    Current Opportunity Status

Example:

NVDA — Saved Research

Research at Plan Creation
Research Score: 67
Technical: 85
Fundamental: 60
Institutional: 50
Classification: Growth Candidate
Market Regime: strong_bull
Created: <timestamp>

Current Opportunity Status
No Longer Qualified

NVDA is not present in the latest qualified-candidate snapshot.

Do not display "Research Not Available" when saved research evidence exists.

4. REVIEW RESEARCH WORKFLOW

The Review Research action must work even when the symbol is no longer
qualified.

The review page should compare:

SAVED / AT CREATION
versus
CURRENT

Current may legitimately contain:

Qualification: No Longer Qualified
Ranking: Not Ranked
Latest candidate score: 0 or unavailable
Removal reason: no longer meets qualification criteria

The user should be able to acknowledge the material change.

IMPORTANT:

Acknowledging the review must NOT automatically make the plan executable.

If the underlying thesis no longer meets qualification requirements,
Execution Preflight should continue to block execution appropriately.

5. EXECUTION PREFLIGHT

Update/verify preflight behavior for this case.

Expected:

Trade Plan                 PASS
Research Lifecycle         REQUIRES_REVIEW or FAIL
Plan Freshness             REQUIRES_REVIEW
Broker Connection          PASS
Broker Account             PASS
Quote Validation           independently evaluated
Structure Validation       PASS

Blocker should be something like:

[PLAN_REQUIRES_REVIEW]
NVDA no longer qualifies under the current research methodology.
Review current research conditions before proceeding.

After review acknowledgment, DO NOT automatically PASS lifecycle if the
current methodology says the candidate remains unqualified.

The lifecycle engine must distinguish:

review has been performed

from:

research thesis currently qualifies.

6. DATA INTEGRITY

Do not mutate the immutable research-at-creation snapshot when current
Opportunity Intelligence changes.

Historical plan evidence must remain historically accurate.

Do not create fake/current candidate records merely to make navigation work.

7. NAVIGATION

Audit these paths:

Trade Plans -> NVDA -> Open
Trade Plan -> Open Research Workspace
Trade Plan -> Review Research
Research -> Trade Plan
Execution Preflight -> Trade Plan / Research links

All must handle a saved plan whose symbol is no longer currently qualified.

No route should assume:

current candidate exists == saved plan exists

8. UI LANGUAGE

Keep language research-oriented and compliance-safe.

Use:
"No Longer Qualified"
"Research Review Required"
"Current research conditions changed"
"Review current evidence"

Do not use:
"Sell"
"Exit now"
"Close position"
"Bad trade"
"Do not buy"

This lifecycle system describes research state, not personalized investment
instructions.

TESTS

B. Qualified candidate -> create Trade Plan -> candidate later removed
C. Removed candidate -> saved Trade Plan remains accessible
D. Removed candidate -> historical research remains accessible
E. Removed candidate -> Review Research works
F. Removed candidate -> Execution Preflight blocks appropriately

Run existing relevant tests plus new regression tests.

Do not modify unrelated Opportunity Intelligence ranking methodology.

Using the EXISTING saved NVDA Trade Plan:

2. It never says "Trade plan not found" while the saved plan exists.

4. Saved research-at-creation evidence remains visible.
5. Open Research Workspace works and shows historical research when current

6. Review Research works.

8. No historical snapshot is overwritten.
9. No fake current NVDA candidate is created.
10. Existing tests and new regression tests pass.
